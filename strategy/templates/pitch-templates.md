## 8. Festival Application Bio (Long -- 250 words)

```
All Points North is a progressive rock band from Uden, the Netherlands, 
combining complex compositions with emotional depth. The five-piece 
formation -- two guitars, bass, drums, and vocals with three-part harmonies 
including drummer backing vocals -- creates a sound that balances technical 
precision with accessible melodic songwriting.

Influenced by Tool, Haken, TesseracT, Oceansize, Karnivool, and Porcupine 
Tree, All Points North explores the boundaries of progressive rock and metal 
while maintaining the atmospheric storytelling that draws listeners into 
extended compositions. Their music is characterized by complex time 
signatures, polyrhythmic structures, dynamic contrasts, and a blend of heavy 
riffs with delicate, atmospheric passages.

The band's debut EP "Embracer" (January 2026) features four tracks that 
showcase the full range of their musical identity. Lead single "Sargassum" 
is a multi-section progressive journey, while "Ready for the Fall," "Suffer 
in Silence," and "Here I Am" explore different corners of the progressive 
rock spectrum. The EP has been featured on independent progressive rock 
playlists and reviewed by [publication, if available].

Live, All Points North delivers high-energy performances with technical 
precision and visual engagement. With 15+ shows across the Netherlands and 
a growing presence in the Dutch progressive underground, the band is 
building a dedicated fanbase through authentic music that places artistic 
integrity above commercial considerations.

All Points North is available for festival slots, headline shows, and 
support slots. Set length: 30-60 minutes (flexible).
```
