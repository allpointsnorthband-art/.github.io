## 2. Spotify Canvas -- Create for Every Track

Canvas is a looping 3-8 second video that plays behind your track on mobile. Tracks with Canvas get **5-10% more streams** and **145% more share-to-story actions**.

### How to Create (Free)

**Option A: Canva (easiest)**
1. Open canva.com -- create a free account
2. Create a design: 1080x1920px (9:16 vertical)
3. Use the "Animate" feature on a static image to create subtle motion (zoom, pan, fade)
4. Export as MP4, 3-8 seconds, under 16MB
5. Upload via Spotify for Artists > Music > Select Track > Canvas

**Option B: CapCut (more creative control)**
1. Import album art or live footage
2. Trim to 3-8 seconds
3. Add subtle effects: slow zoom, color shift, particle overlay
4. Export 1080x1920, MP4

### Canvas Ideas for Each Embracer Track

| Track | Canvas Concept |
|---|---|
| Sargassum | Slow pan across textured ocean/seaweed imagery, dark blue tones |
| Ready for the Fall | Time-lapse of clouds or falling leaves, muted warm palette |
| Suffer in Silence | Close-up of guitar strings vibrating, desaturated with grain |
| Here I Am | Live footage clip -- the most energetic moment from a show |

### Technical Requirements
- Format: MP4 or JPEG (for static)
- Dimensions: 1080 x 1920 (9:16)
- Duration: 3-8 seconds (loops automatically)
- Max file size: 16MB
- No text overlays (Spotify may reject)

## 4. Spotify Editorial Pitch Guide

Spotify offers a **free** editorial pitch tool for unreleased music. This is the single most underused free tool available to independent artists. It gives you a direct line to Spotify's editorial team.

### When to Pitch
- Available **only for unreleased tracks** -- you must pitch at least 7 days before release (ideally 3-4 weeks)
- Pitch exactly ONE track per release (your strongest single)

### How to Pitch (Step by Step)

1. Open Spotify for Artists (artists.spotify.com)
2. Go to **Music** > **Upcoming**
3. Select the unreleased track
4. Click **"Submit a song"**
5. Fill in EVERY field:

**Required fields:**
- Song language
- Is this a cover? Is this instrumental?
- Genre(s): Select "Progressive Rock" primary, add "Progressive Metal" and "Alternative Metal"
- Subgenre(s): "Post-Metal," "Art Rock," "Technical Metal"
- Mood(s): "Atmospheric," "Intense," "Complex," "Dark," "Energetic"
- Song description (FREE TEXT -- this is your pitch): Write 2-3 sentences explaining what makes this track special. Mention the story behind it, unusual musical elements, or production choices. Be specific and human.
- Culture/style: "Progressive," "Underground"
- Instruments: Guitar, Bass, Drums, Vocals

**Example pitch text:**
> "Sargassum" is a 7-minute progressive rock journey that moves from delicate clean guitar arpeggios into crushing polyrhythmic riffs. Inspired by the ecological collapse of Atlantic sargassum seaweed, the track features three-part vocal harmonies over shifting time signatures. All Points North blends the atmospheric depth of Tool with the technical precision of Haken, creating something that rewards deep listening. This is the opening track from our debut EP "Embracer" and represents the most ambitious piece of music we've written.

### Pitch Success Tips
- Complete metadata = higher chance of editorial placement
- Use specific, descriptive language -- not "this song is great"
- Reference comparable artists Spotify already knows
- Describe the listener experience, not just the music theory

## 5. Algorithmic Playlist Triggers

Spotify's algorithmic playlists (Discover Weekly, Release Radar, Radio, Autoplay) are driven by three signals. Here is how to optimize each.

### Signal 1: Listener Behavior

**What matters (in order of importance):**
1. **Save rate** -- listeners clicking the heart/save button
2. **Playlist add rate** -- listeners adding the track to their own playlists
3. **Completion rate** -- listeners finishing the track (critical for prog: long tracks have inherently lower completion rates)
4. **Repeat listens** -- listeners playing the track again within 24 hours
5. **Skip rate** -- listeners skipping within the first 30 seconds (negative signal)

**What to do:**
- Always use "Save this track" as your CTA on social media, never "Stream this track"
- On Instagram Stories, use the "Add to playlist" Spotify sticker
- Never ask people to just "play" the song in the background -- passive streams with low engagement hurt more than they help
- For long tracks (6+ min), ensure the first 30 seconds are compelling enough to prevent skips

### Signal 2: Audio Similarity

Spotify's audio analysis engine fingerprints every track for tempo, energy, acousticness, valence, danceability, and instrumentation. It then recommends tracks with similar fingerprints.

**What to do:**
- You cannot change the audio fingerprint, but you CAN influence which "buckets" Spotify places you in through metadata (genre tags, playlist context)
- Ensure your tracks are on playlists alongside artists you want to be associated with (your own curated playlists help here)

### Signal 3: Listener Graph (Collaborative Filtering)

Spotify tracks which listeners play which artists. If many listeners who play Tool also play APN, Spotify's algorithm will recommend APN to other Tool listeners.

**What to do:**
- Your curated playlists (Section 3) directly feed this signal
- Cross-promote with bands who share your target audience
- Avoid promotion to audiences outside your genre (e.g., playlist-exchange groups with random genres) -- this pollutes your listener graph

### The 48-Hour Window

The first 48 hours after a release are critical. Spotify tests your track with existing followers via Release Radar. Strong early engagement = exponential algorithmic push.
