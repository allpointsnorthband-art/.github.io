# Content Engine -- Instagram Reels Strategy & Workflow

**Band**: All Points North
**Primary platform**: Instagram Reels
**Time budget**: 1 hour/week (batch creation)

---

## 1. Why Instagram Reels (Not TikTok)

- The progressive rock audience skews 25-45 years old -- Instagram's core demographic
- TikTok's audience skews younger and favors pop/hip-hop/electronic genres
- Instagram Reels persist in the Explore tab longer than TikTok posts
- Instagram has built-in Spotify integration (music stickers, direct links)
- One platform done well beats three done poorly -- especially at 1-3 hrs/week

**Expand to TikTok and YouTube Shorts later** by repurposing the same Reels content (identical format works on all three platforms).

---

## 2. Content Pillars -- The Three Formats

Every piece of content falls into one of three categories. Rotate between them for variety.

### Format A: "Riff of the Week" (Performance Content)

**What**: 15-30 second clip of someone playing a riff, bass line, drum fill, or vocal line from an APN track or new material being written.

**Why it works**: The prog/metal community is musician-heavy. They engage deeply with technical playing content. This is also the easiest content to create -- just point a phone at the player.

**How to shoot**:
1. Phone on a tripod or propped against something stable
2. Landscape or portrait (portrait performs better on Reels)
3. Good audio matters: use the phone's mic close to the amp, or record audio separately and sync
4. One take is fine -- imperfect is authentic
5. 15-30 seconds max

**Caption formula**:
> "[Instrument emoji] This riff from [Track Name] in [time signature] always hits different. What's your favorite moment on Embracer? [music emoji]"

**Audio**: Use the APN track from Spotify as the Reel's audio (this creates an attribution link -- anyone who taps the audio name goes to the Spotify track).

**Hashtags** (save as a preset):
```
#progressiverock #progrock #progmetal #guitarriff #bassguitar #drums #technicalmusic #allpointsnorth #embracer #dutchrock #newmusic2026 #progrockband #hakenband #toolband #progressivemetal
```

### Format B: "Behind the Music" (Process Content)

**What**: Raw, unpolished footage from rehearsal, writing sessions, the studio, load-in at a venue, or band life. The "making of" is more engaging than the "finished product."

**Why it works**: Fans connect with the human side. Seeing the work behind the music builds loyalty and makes people root for the band.

**Ideas**:
- 15-second clip of the band working through a new riff in the rehearsal room
- Phone propped up catching a spontaneous creative moment
- Quick band discussion about a song arrangement (subtitled)
- Loading gear into a van
- Soundcheck at a venue
- Celebrating finishing a track

**Caption formula**:
> "Writing session from last night -- this one's turning into something special. [eyes emoji] New material coming. #allpointsnorth #rehearsal #newmusic #writingsession"

**Audio**: Either original audio from the clip, or an APN track layered underneath.

### Format C: "Gear & Tone" (Educational Content)

**What**: Show a pedal, amp setting, guitar, tuning, or technique used in APN's music. Explain it briefly.

**Why it works**: The prog/metal community is obsessed with gear. Gear content gets shared, saved, and commented on at extremely high rates. This is also evergreen -- it stays relevant for months.

**Ideas**:
- "How we get the tone on [Track Name]" -- walk through the signal chain
- "This pedal changed everything" -- close-up of a pedal with the APN riff over it
- "The tuning we use for Embracer" -- show the tuning, play a riff
- "Our drummer's kit setup" -- tour of the drum kit
- "Three-part vocal harmonies: how we do it live" -- show the vocal setup

**Caption formula**:
> "[gear emoji] The secret behind the [Track Name] tone: [Pedal Name] into [Amp]. Cranked gain, rolled-back volume. What's your go-to tone? [pointing down emoji]"

**Audio**: APN track demonstrating the gear being discussed.

## 4. Batch Creation Workflow

**Do this every Sunday. Total time: 45 minutes.**

### Step 1: Shoot (20 minutes)

At rehearsal (or at home with an instrument):
- Record 3-4 short clips (15-30 seconds each)
- Mix formats: 1 performance clip, 1 behind-the-scenes, 1 gear close-up
- Use portrait orientation (9:16)
- Natural lighting is fine -- don't overthink production quality
- Record more than you need -- you'll pick the best 2-3

### Step 2: Edit (15 minutes)

In CapCut (free, mobile or desktop):
- Trim each clip to 15-30 seconds
- Add text overlay if needed (song name, gear name)
- If using an APN track as audio: mute original audio, add the Spotify track
- No fancy transitions needed -- straight cuts are fine for authenticity
- Export at 1080x1920

### Step 3: Schedule (10 minutes)

In Meta Business Suite (free):
1. Open business.facebook.com > Content > Create
2. Upload each Reel
3. Write caption (use formulas above)
4. Add hashtags (paste from saved preset)
5. Schedule: Tuesday 7PM CET, Thursday 7PM CET
6. Create one Story for Saturday (poll or question sticker)

## 5. Meta Business Suite Scheduling Guide

### Initial Setup (One-time, 15 minutes)

1. Download Meta Business Suite (mobile app or use business.facebook.com)
2. Connect the Instagram account (@allpointsnorth.band) to a Facebook Page (/apnlive)
3. Switch to "Professional Account" on Instagram (Settings > Account > Switch to Professional)
4. Select "Musician/Band" as the category
5. Meta Business Suite now controls both Instagram and Facebook from one dashboard

### Scheduling a Reel

1. Open Meta Business Suite
2. Click **Create** > **Reel**
3. Upload the edited video
4. Add caption + hashtags
5. Toggle "Share to Facebook" if desired
6. Click **Schedule** and pick date/time
7. Best posting times for European prog audience: **Tuesday 7PM CET**, **Thursday 7PM CET**, **Saturday 12PM CET**

### Scheduling a Story

1. Open Meta Business Suite
2. Click **Create** > **Story**
3. Upload image or short video
4. Add stickers (poll, question, music, link)
5. Schedule for Saturday

## 7. Engagement Routine (5 min/day or 15 min 3x/week)

Posting content is only half the equation. Engagement builds community.

**Daily quick actions (pick 2-3):**
- Reply to every comment on your posts (within 24 hours)
- Like and comment on 5 posts from similar bands or prog community accounts
- Reply to DMs
- Engage with posts tagged #progressiverock or #progmetal in Explore

**Weekly:**
- Follow 10-15 accounts in the prog/metal community (fans, bands, bloggers, playlist curators)
- Reshare fan content or mentions to Stories

**The 90/10 rule**: 90% of your engagement should be genuine community interaction (commenting on others' posts, joining conversations). 10% is promoting your own music. This builds organic reach.

Once Instagram is running smoothly (after ~2 months), repurpose the same content:

| Source | Repurpose To | Effort |
|---|---|---|

| Instagram Reel | YouTube Shorts | Re-upload same video, add to channel | 
| Instagram Reel | Facebook Reel | Auto-shared via Meta Business Suite |
| Instagram Story | Facebook Story | Auto-shared via Meta Business Suite |

**YouTube-specific addition**: Upload full tracks as "Audio" with a static album art image or visualizer. These are indexed by Google and remain searchable indefinitely. This is free, evergreen discovery.
