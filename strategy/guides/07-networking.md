## 2. Cross-Promotion Playbook

### Step 1: Make First Contact (Not About You)

Before asking for anything, build a genuine relationship:

1. Follow the band on Instagram and Spotify
2. Save/playlist their tracks (this genuinely helps them)
3. Comment on their posts with substance (not "great track!" but "that polyrhythmic section at 3:24 is devastating")
4. Share their music to your Stories with a genuine endorsement
5. Attend their shows if geographically possible

**Do this for 2-4 weeks before making any ask.**

### Step 2: Propose Mutual Value

After building rapport, propose something where BOTH bands benefit:

**Show Swap Proposal:**
```
Hey [Band],

We're All Points North from Uden -- really digging your latest release. 
We're in a similar lane (progressive rock/metal, 5-piece, technical but 
melodic).

Would you be up for a show swap? We could set up a double bill at a 
venue in [your city], and you could join us at a venue in [our area]. 
Both bands bring their crowd, venue gets a bigger draw, everyone wins.

What do you think?

-- [Name], All Points North
```

**Shared Playlist Proposal:**
```
Hey [Band],

We're putting together a "Dutch Progressive Underground" Spotify playlist 
featuring the best emerging prog bands from the Netherlands. We'd love to 
include [their track name] alongside our music.

The idea is to cross-promote -- all bands share the playlist, everyone 
benefits from the combined audience. We'll add 2-3 of your tracks.

Interested?
```

**Instagram Collab Proposal:**
```
Hey [Band],

Quick idea: what if we did a shared Reel where our guitarist plays your 
riff and your guitarist plays ours? Tag each other, both audiences see it.

Seen this format blow up for prog bands on Instagram. Down to try it?
```

### Step 3: Execute and Follow Through

- **Show swaps**: Agree on dates, both bands promote to their respective audiences, share the bill, split costs/revenue fairly
- **Shared playlists**: Create the playlist, add tracks from all participating bands, all bands share it
- **Social content**: Plan, create, and cross-post simultaneously for maximum reach

## 3. Shared Playlist Strategy: "Dutch Prog Underground"

### Creating the Playlist

1. Create a Spotify playlist: "Dutch Progressive Underground -- 2026"
2. Write a keyword-rich description:
   > "The best emerging progressive rock and metal from the Netherlands. Featuring All Points North, Hesken, CiLiCe, and more. Updated weekly. Curated by the Dutch prog community."
3. Add 30-50 tracks from 10-15 Dutch prog/metal bands
4. Include 3-4 APN tracks spread throughout (not at the top)
5. Create a branded cover image in Canva

### Growing the Playlist

- Ask every band on the playlist to share it with their audience
- Post to Instagram Stories weekly: "This week's update on the Dutch Prog Underground playlist"
- Submit to r/SpotifyPlaylists and r/progmetal
- Add new Dutch bands as you discover them -- this makes you the curator/hub of the scene

### Benefits

- Positions APN as a scene builder, not just another band seeking attention
- Creates goodwill with every band included
- Drives genuine Spotify algorithmic association between APN and established prog acts
- Gives you a reason to reach out to any Dutch prog band: "We'd like to add you to our playlist"

## 4. Live Networking Tactics

### At Shows (Your Own or Others')

- Introduce yourself to the other bands on the bill -- exchange contacts
- Offer to help with load-in/load-out (builds goodwill fast)
- Take photos/videos of other bands and tag them on socials (they'll return the favor)
- Exchange "we should play a show together" conversations with actual follow-up emails within 48 hours

### At Festivals

- Same as above, multiplied. Festivals are THE networking opportunity.
- Bring business cards or a phone with the EPK QR code ready
- Watch other bands' sets -- have specific compliments ready (not generic "you were great")

## 5. Opening for Larger Acts

The fastest way to gain exposure is opening for a larger band at a venue show. Here's how:

### Finding Opportunities

- Check local venue calendars for incoming prog/metal acts without announced openers
- Contact the venue directly: "We'd love to open for [Band] on [Date]. We're local, we'll bring extra audience, and we have a professional tech rider."
- Contact the touring band's booking agent (often listed on their website): "Would you accept a local opener for your [City] date?"
- Bands that play Doornroosje, Effenaar, or 013 often need local support acts
