## 3. Press Outreach Strategy

### Timing

Press outreach should be tied to **news hooks**:
- New release (single or EP): Pitch 2-3 weeks before release for premieres, day-of for reviews
- Show announcement (especially festivals): Pitch around the announcement
- Music video release: Pitch for premiere
- Milestone: "Our EP just hit X streams" or "We just played ProgPower" -- only if genuinely noteworthy

Do NOT pitch "just because." Editors receive hundreds of emails. Give them a reason.

### Outreach Cadence

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| 3-4 weeks before release | Pitch Spotify editorial (Spotify for Artists) |
| 2-3 weeks before release | Pitch blogs for premiere (exclusive first-play of a single) |
| Release week | Send review copies to all Tier 1 and Tier 2 blogs |
| 1 week after release | Follow up once (and only once) with blogs that didn't respond |
| Ongoing | Submit to SubmitHub when new tracks release |

### Premiere Strategy

A "premiere" is when a blog publishes your track or video before/on release day as an exclusive. This is valuable because:
- The blog promotes it to their audience (free promotion)
- It creates a backlink to your music (SEO value)
- It adds credibility ("as premiered on The Progspace")

**How to pitch a premiere:**
```
Subject: Exclusive premiere offer: All Points North -- "[Track Name]" (progressive rock)

Hi [Name],

We're offering an exclusive premiere of our new single "[Track Name]" 
releasing [Date]. Would [Publication Name] be interested in debuting it?

We can provide: streaming link (private until release), press photos, 
band bio, and a track-by-track commentary.

Quick listen: [private SoundCloud or unlisted YouTube link]

Let me know if this fits your editorial calendar.

[Name]
All Points North
```

## 4. SubmitHub Strategy for Press

SubmitHub isn't just for playlists -- it also connects to music blogs.

### Blog Submissions on SubmitHub

1. Go to submithub.com > Submit
2. Filter by Type: **Blog**
3. Filter by Genre: Progressive Rock, Progressive Metal, Post-Metal, Post-Rock
4. Sort by Approval Rate (descending)
5. Target blogs with 15%+ approval rate
6. Write a personalized note for each (reference a recent review they published)

### Budget Allocation (When Funds Are Available)

For a new single release:
- 5 premium blog submissions ($1-2 each) = $5-10
- 5 premium playlist submissions ($1-2 each) = $5-10
- Total per release: $10-20

This is the single best use of a small budget.

## 5. Press Kit Essentials

Ensure these are ready before any outreach:

- [ ] Short bio (100 words)
- [ ] Long bio (250 words)
- [ ] 3-5 high-res press photos (300 DPI, landscape and portrait)
- [ ] Album artwork (high-res)
- [ ] Streaming links (Spotify, Bandcamp, Apple Music, YouTube)
- [ ] EPK URL
- [ ] One-sheet (single page PDF with bio, photo, links, stats)
- [ ] Contact info (email, phone, social links)

All of these exist on the EPK website except the one-sheet PDF. Consider creating one in Canva (free).
