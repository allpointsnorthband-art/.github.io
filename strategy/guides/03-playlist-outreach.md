# Playlist Outreach -- Curator Research & Pitching Guide

**Band**: All Points North
**Time budget**: 30 min/week
**Goal**: Get placed on 15-30 independent playlists in 12 months

---

## 1. How Playlist Pitching Works

There are two types of Spotify playlists:

1. **Editorial playlists** -- curated by Spotify's editorial team. Pitched via Spotify for Artists (covered in the Spotify Engine guide). Free, but competitive.
2. **Independent playlists** -- curated by regular users, bloggers, or small labels. These are your primary target. Curators add tracks they genuinely like, and many are open to submissions.

Independent playlists with 200-5,000 followers are the sweet spot: large enough to drive streams, small enough that curators actually read submissions.

---

## 2. Finding the Right Playlists

### Method 1: Spotify Search

1. Open Spotify desktop app
2. Search for genre terms: "progressive rock," "prog metal," "post-metal," "new prog"
3. Filter to "Playlists"
4. Look for playlists that are NOT "Made for you" or "By Spotify" -- these are independent
5. Check follower count (visible on desktop)
6. Target: 200-5,000 followers
7. Check if the playlist has been recently updated (last 1-2 weeks)
8. Note the curator's name -- you'll need this for outreach

### Method 2: "Fans Also Like" Trail

1. Go to a similar artist's Spotify page (e.g., Haken, Leprous, Caligula's Horse)
2. Scroll to "Discovered On" -- this shows playlists where that artist appears
3. Click through to each playlist
4. If the playlist is independently curated and in your genre, add it to your target list

### Method 3: SubmitHub Discovery

1. Go to submithub.com
2. Browse curators by genre: Progressive Rock, Progressive Metal, Post-Rock
3. Filter by type: Spotify Playlist
4. Note curators who accept free submissions
5. Check their approval rate (aim for curators with 15%+ approval)

### Method 4: Reddit & Forums

- r/SpotifyPlaylists -- curators post their playlists here
- r/progmetal and r/progrockmusic -- look for "playlist" threads
- Search for "progressive rock playlist" on Reddit

## 3. The Pitch Template

### For DMs (Instagram/Twitter)

Keep it short. Curators get dozens of submissions. Respect their time.

```
Hey [Name], love the [Playlist Name] playlist -- [Artist X] into [Artist Y] 
is a great sequence. We're All Points North, a prog rock band from the 
Netherlands. Our track "[Track Name]" would fit well between [Artist A] and 
[Artist B] on your list.

Here's the track: [Spotify Link]

No pressure at all -- just thought it might be a good match. Thanks for 
curating great music.
```

### For Email

```
Subject: Playlist submission -- All Points North (progressive rock)

Hi [Name],

I found your playlist "[Playlist Name]" and it's right in our lane -- love 
the mix of [Artist X] and [Artist Y].

We're All Points North, a 5-piece progressive rock band from the Netherlands. 
Our track "[Track Name]" blends atmospheric passages with heavy polyrhythmic 
riffs -- think Tool meets Haken with three-part vocal harmonies.

Spotify: [Link]
EPK: https://allpointsnorthband-art.github.io/.github.io/

If it fits your list, we'd be honored. Either way, thanks for supporting 
independent music.

Cheers,
[Your Name]
All Points North
```

### What NOT to Do

- Do not send a generic mass message with no playlist name mentioned
- Do not ask for "a favor" or sound desperate
- Do not send follow-up messages if they don't respond -- move on
- Do not offer payment (this violates Spotify ToS and curators hate it)
- Do not pitch more than one track at a time
- Do not pitch to playlists that don't match your genre (it wastes everyone's time and pollutes your listener graph)

## 4. SubmitHub Guide

### Free Credits

- SubmitHub gives you 2 free credits when you sign up
- You earn additional free credits by reviewing other artists' submissions
- Free submissions have a response window of up to 20 days
- Response rate on free credits is <10%, but it costs nothing

### Using Free Credits Effectively

1. Go to submithub.com > Submit
2. Upload your track (must be on Spotify/Apple Music)
3. Filter curators: Genre = Progressive Rock / Progressive Metal / Post-Rock
4. Sort by "Approval Rate" (descending)
5. Select curators with 15%+ approval rate
6. Write a personal note for each curator (do NOT use the same generic note)
7. Submit

### Premium Credits (When Budget Allows)

- Premium credits cost $1-2 each
- Curators must respond within 48 hours (guaranteed feedback)
- Approval rate is significantly higher (~30% platform average)
- Budget recommendation when funds are available: $10-20/release = 10-15 submissions

## 5. Pitch Tracking Spreadsheet

Create this Google Sheet and update it weekly:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Playlist Name | Name of the target playlist |
| Curator | Curator's name or handle |
| Followers | Playlist follower count |
| Contact | Email, Instagram DM, SubmitHub, etc. |
| Track Pitched | Which APN track you submitted |
| Date Pitched | When you sent the pitch |
| Status | Pending / Accepted / Declined / No Response |
| Notes | Any feedback received |

## 6. Weekly Outreach Routine (30 min)

### Wednesday Playlist Pitching Block

| Minute | Action |
|---|---|
| 0-10 | Research: Find 3-5 new playlists using the methods above. Add to tracker. |
| 10-25 | Pitch: Send personalized messages to 3-5 curators. Log in tracker. |
| 25-30 | Follow up: Check status of previous submissions. Update tracker. |

### Monthly Review

At the end of each month:
- How many playlists were you added to?
- What is your acceptance rate? (Target: 15-25%)
- Which tracks are getting accepted most? (Double down on pitching those)
- Which playlist sizes are converting to actual streams? (Focus there)

---

## 7. Track Selection Strategy

Not every track is equally "pitchable." Choose the right track for the right playlist.

| Track | Best Pitch For | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sargassum | Long-form prog playlists, "deep listening" playlists | Likely the most ambitious/complex track |
| Ready for the Fall | General prog rock playlists, "new releases" | Accessible entry point |
| Suffer in Silence | Post-metal, heavy prog, atmospheric playlists | Heavier, darker mood |
| Here I Am | Energy playlists, live-feeling playlists | Likely the most energetic track |

Pitch **one track per curator**. If accepted, you can suggest additional tracks later.

---

## 8. After Getting Placed

When a curator adds your track:

1. **Thank them** -- send a brief, genuine thank-you message
2. **Share it** -- post the playlist to your Instagram Story, tag the curator
3. **Follow the playlist** -- and encourage your followers to follow it too
4. **Track the streams** -- monitor in Spotify for Artists whether this playlist is driving streams
5. **Build the relationship** -- curators who add you once are likely to add future releases too. Keep them in your contacts.
